## Banner — marmotte-chatui-banner.png

Use case: identity-preserve. Asset: wide transparent README brand banner for the .NET/Blazor chat project Marmotte.ChatUi. Image 1 is the primary edit target: the turquoise smiling marmot leaning over a wooden sign, cream muzzle, brown nose, large white incisors, red scarf with white alpine/Swiss motifs, rich illustrated fur and warm wooden texture. Image 2 is supporting character-identity reference only. Preserve the original mascot identity, palette, scarf, warm friendly expression, high-quality illustrative style and wooden sign composition. Adapt the original banner to this product. Replace the main sign text with exactly "Marmotte.ChatUi", clearly and accurately rendered; remove "MarmotteDB" and remove the entire small database/Rust tagline. Do not add any slogan or secondary text. Keep the overall wide balanced silhouette, mascot on the left above the sign, fully visible without cropping, comfortable transparent margin. Retain the mountain ornament and tasteful turquoise accents if they fit. Transparent background with actual alpha, clean edges, no rectangular backdrop, no checkerboard baked into art, no watermark. The output is one finished standalone banner, not a mockup or asset sheet.

## Compact mascot — marmotte-chatui-mascot.png

Use case: identity-preserve. Asset: compact square application mascot for Marmotte.ChatUi on a genuinely transparent background. Both provided images are identity references: use the same turquoise marmot, cream muzzle, brown nose, two white incisors, red scarf with white alpine/Swiss motifs, and the same polished hand-painted cartoon illustration style. Create one compact head-and-upper-body portrait with small paws visible, a friendly welcoming expression and eyes open. Preserve the recognizable character and palette; do not invent a different animal. Keep the silhouette clear and readable at small sizes, centered with transparent padding. No wooden sign, no letters or text, no goggles, no laboratory flask or other props, no badge border, no background, no watermark. Full head, ears and scarf must fit in frame. Output one standalone icon/mascot, not multiple variants or a sheet.
